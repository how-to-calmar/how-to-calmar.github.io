## Reuse notes

If you reuse a third-party image, follow the source licence rather than assuming the repository licence applies. Keep the creator, source, licence link and modification statement together. For clinical or research use, also consult the source paper: a teaching thumbnail does not preserve the acquisition details or context needed for interpretation.
