## Neurodesk and learning

- [NeurodeskEDU](https://neurodesk.org/edu/intro.html) provides static tutorials and executable examples.
- [NeurodeskEDU source](https://github.com/neurodesk/neurodeskedu) shows how examples are structured, tested, and published.
- [Neurodesk documentation](https://neurodesk.org/docs/) covers supported environments and reproducible containers.
- Renton et al. describe Neurodesk's portable and reproducible environment in [Nature Methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-02145-x).

## Standards and open data

- [BIDS](https://bids.neuroimaging.io/) standardises neuroimaging data and metadata organisation.
- [OpenNeuro](https://openneuro.org/) hosts public BIDS datasets.
- The [Aphasia Recovery Cohort](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-024-03819-7) is an open chronic-stroke resource used in CALMaR-related work.
- [ATLAS](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01401-7) provides curated stroke MRI and lesion masks.
- [ISLES](https://isles-challenge.org/) provides acute and subacute stroke lesion-segmentation benchmarks.

## Lesion methods and interpretation

- MacIntosh and Graham review MRI contrasts used to visualise stroke and recovery: [Frontiers in Neurology](https://doi.org/10.3389/fneur.2013.00060).
- Pustina et al. describe LINDA for chronic stroke lesion segmentation: [Human Brain Mapping](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.23110).
- Wilson and Hula review multivariate approaches to aphasia and neural substrates: [Current Neurology and Neuroscience Reports](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11910-019-0971-6).
- Salvalaggio et al. compare lesion and disconnection information for post-stroke deficit prediction: [Brain](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awaa156).
- Boyd et al. distinguish developmental and trial-ready stroke recovery biomarkers: [Neurorehabilitation and Neural Repair](https://doi.org/10.1177/1545968317732680).
- Nichols et al. summarise MRI analysis and sharing practices: [Nature Neuroscience](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4500).

## Language systems

- Hickok and Poeppel describe a dorsal–ventral model of cortical speech processing: [Nature Reviews Neuroscience](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2113).
- Fedorenko, Ivanova and Regev distinguish the core language-selective network from interacting perceptual, motor and broader cognitive systems: [Nature Reviews Neuroscience](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-024-00802-4).
